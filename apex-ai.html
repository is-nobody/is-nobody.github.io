Why Apex AI?

Blazing Fast

O(1) hash table lookups with inverted word index and query caching. Multi-stage search in microseconds. Pure C.

Smart Matching

Exact and fuzzy matching with typos, keyword extraction, stop word filtering, topic context awareness, and Unicode normalization.

Tiny & Portable

Single binary. Zero dependencies. Runs anywhere.

Privacy First

One `.c` file per topic. Drop a file, rebuild, done.
